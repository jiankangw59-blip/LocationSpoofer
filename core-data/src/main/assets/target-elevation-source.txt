Elevation source and attribution

Surface estimates: Copernicus DEM GLO-90 (approximately 90 m), obtained via Open-Meteo. Heights are originally relative to the EGM2008 geoid and are converted locally to WGS84 ellipsoid heights using the Android altitude model. No claim of measured handset, building-floor or bare-earth altitude is made.
